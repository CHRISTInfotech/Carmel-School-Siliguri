K.E. Carmel School

Facilities

A well-maintained, student-friendly campus that supports academic learning and overall development—providing essential facilities for a comfortable and effective learning experience.

On campus
  • Spacious classrooms
  • Computer lab
  • Library
  • Science facilities

Classrooms

  • Spacious and well-ventilated
  • Clean and organized learning environment
  • Designed to support focused and interactive learning

Laboratories

  • Well-equipped with modern instruments and equipment
  • Safe and practical environment for hands-on learning
  • Encourages scientific exploration and innovation
  • The Computer Lab, Science Labs and Library must be used with utmost care.
  • If anything is found missing or damaged, students will have to share the expenses in common.
  • Students must not open the CPU or unplug the mouse from the computers.

Library

  • Wide range of books collection and learning resources
  • Quiet and comfortable space for reading and research
  • Promotes knowledge, curiosity, and lifelong learning
  • Students may borrow only one book at a time and retain it for one week. Borrowed books are not transferable.
  • If a book is not returned on the due date a fine of Rs. 5/- per day thereafter is imposed.
  • Reference books, periodicals, etc. should not be taken out of the reading room.
  • Borrowers must report any damage, loss of pages, pictures etc. to the Librarian before they accept the books. If any book is lost, damaged or badly handled, the actual cost for the replacement of the book will have to be paid by the borrower. No page shall be mutilated, torn or spoilt.
  • Strict silence should be maintained inside the Library and Reading Room.

Sports & Playground

  • Open space for outdoor games and activities
  • Encouragement for physical fitness and teamwork
  • Participation in sports and recreational activities

Transport & Safety

  • Reliable transportation with well-maintained buses
  • Focus on student safety and well-being
  • Route coverage designed for student convenience
Other Facilities

School Assembly

Co-Curricular Activities

K.E.Carmel school believes in the integral formation of the children and insists on values, morals, character and skill development of all the students. To develop the varied interests and different talents of pupils, we provide ample opportunity through several co-curricular activities. Besides academic training, the students are given opportunity to get involved in artistic, cultural and social activities through various clubs.
