Picnics and Study Tours

10.1. Picnics and Study tours are a part of the study and class activity and hence all are expected to join. It will encourage and strengthen friendliness and love for each other. It is also a source of relaxation and helps to widen knowledge, good relations, social values, maturity and curiosity.

10.2. All the divisions of each standard will plan together and finalize the destination, date and expenditure with the approval of the Principal. Class teachers have to organise the programme.

Co-Curricular Activities

K.E.Carmel school believes in the integral formation of the children and insists on values, morals, character and skill development of all the students. To develop the varied interests and different talents of pupils, we provide ample opportunity through several co-curricular activities. Besides academic training, the students are given opportunity to get involved in artistic, cultural and social activities through various clubs.
