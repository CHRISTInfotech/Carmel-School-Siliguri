About Us

K.E. Carmel Group of Schools, West Bengal belongs to the Christian Catholic Religious Congregation of Carmelites of Mary Immaculate (C.M.I) Fathers. It is solely owned and managed by C.M.I Dharmanivas Educational Trust (DET) and C.M.I. Dharmaniketan Educational Trust (Eastern Region) under the aegis of Dharmaniketan C.M.I Sub Region, West Bengal of C.M.I St. Joseph’s Province, Trivandrum, Kerala, India. It draws inspiration from Saint Kuriakose Elias Chavara (1805-71), a Saint and Social Reformer of the 19th Century in the state of Kerala, India. He is one of the founders of the C.M.I Congregation, which is the first Catholic Religious Congregation for men in India. Following his vision, C.M.I Congregation has established more than 700 educational institutions all over India and abroad. The most reputed universities, colleges, schools and other educational institutions which are run by C.M.I Congregation in India always stand forth in excellence. The vision of education provided by C.M.I congregation focuses exclusively on the integral development of rural areas of India by providing quality education. K. E. Carmel Group, with this noble vision, flourishes with eight educational institutions in the state of West Bengal. The group is approved as a Minority Educational Institution as F.No.1525 of 2006-143/8. It imparts instructions to students from Nursery to class XII under CISCE curriculum, New Delhi, India.

Being aware that educational process is the most important human resource development, we open our institutions to all, irrespective of sex, caste, colour or faith. We usually prefer Catholics as members of our Minority Community and give them religious formation based on the values of Gospel while others are provided with moral and spiritual formation. We organize various curricular and co-curricular activities for the integral development of our students,

The CMI Missionary Fathers from Kerala (St. Joseph’s Province, Trivandrum) invest their best for the excellent formation of the next generation as our contribution towards the national development. Through our educational institutions we respond to the national need o preparing the youth to be socially responsible so that India becomes a supreme power house by the year 2030. Hence, we select and train committed teachers, who stand by our students in their physical, intellectual, moral, cultural, aesthetic, emotional, spiritual and skill developments. We have been nurturing them with our rich experience in education for the past 189 years upholding the age-old spiritual traditions of India and of the St. Thomas Christians of Apostolic origin.

Hence, we pay attention in forming leaders who are intellectually competent, spiritually mature, morally upright, psychologically integrated, physically healthy, socially acceptable and God-loving who will champion the cause of justice love, truth and peace, and who are ever open to further growth. The secret of our success in education is a community teachers who are committed to their vocation, professionally competent, morally upright, just and humane in dealings and who grow with the nation in the right vision of development. We aspire towards creating a just an humane society w ere dignity o a person is respected unjust social structures are challenged, our cultural heritage of ahimsa, secularism, religious harmony and national integration are to field and where the poor and marginalized are specially attended to.

School History

K.E. Carmel School, Siliguri is part of the K.E. Carmel Group of Schools, West Bengal, which belongs to the Christian Catholic Religious Congregation of Carmelites of Mary Immaculate Fathers. The school is inspired by the educational vision of Saint Kuriakose Elias Chavara, one of the founders of the C.M.I. Congregation and a social reformer of the 19th century. The institution functions under the C.M.I. Dharmaniketan Educational Trust and C.M.I. Dharmaniketan Educational Trust, Eastern Region, under the aegis of Dharmaniketan C.M.I. Sub Region, West Bengal, of C.M.I. St. Joseph’s Province, Trivandrum, Kerala. The C.M.I. Congregation has a long tradition in education and has established many educational institutions across India and abroad. K.E. Carmel Group was founded with the vision of providing quality education, especially with attention to the integral development of students. The group has grown to include eight educational institutions in West Bengal. K.E. Carmel School, Siliguri follows the C.I.S.C.E. / ISC curriculum, New Delhi, and serves students from Nursery to class XII. The school continues the C.M.I. mission of forming students who are intellectually competent, spiritually mature, morally upright, psychologically integrated, physically healthy and socially acceptable. Its motto, “To Plant and Nurture”, reflects its commitment to nurturing young minds through value-based education, discipline, character formation and holistic development.

We imbibe our vision from the educational vision of St. Kurriakose Elias Chavara, our Heavenly Patron. Our Mission

The CMI Mission aims at an education by which character is formed, strength of mind is increased, intellect is expanded and help is extended for one to stand on one’s own feet with a conviction that everyone is a precious gift of God. Hence, K.E. Carmel nurtures the little minds to become: Intellectually competent, spiritually matured, morally upright, psychologically integrated, physically healthy and socially acceptable. K.E. Carmel School is a powerful agent of social change and aspires towards creating a just human society where dignity of the person is respected, unjust social structures are challenged, cultural heritage of Ahimsa, Satya, Secularism, democracy, religious harmony and national integration are upheld and where the poor and marginalised are specially taken care of. Here, young minds are inspired and helped to imbibe genuine human values in the present global context of: Rampant injustice, Religion cultural fundamentalism, Environmental exploitation and the spread of materialistic culture.
