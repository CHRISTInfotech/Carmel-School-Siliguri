His enduring legacy inspires us to nurture every child with knowledge, faith, compassion and a commitment to serve others.

The school follows the ICSE curriculum and encourages every learner to grow through academics, creativity, sports, leadership and service.

Leadership messages

From The
Principal’s Desk

Welcome to K.E. Carmel School — a home away from home, set far from the chaos of the city, embraced by clean air and lush greenery.

Under the loving guidance of the CMI Fathers, we nurture each child not as a name on a roll, but as a unique individual with their own gifts, dreams, and potential. Our mission is simple yet profound: to help every child discover who they truly are, and to help every family witness that discovery unfold.

Education here is not just about academics — it is about character, values, and identity. It is about giving every child room to grow, question, create, and shine.

We welcome you to be part of this journey, where nature nurtures, values guide, and every child finds their own light.

With warm wishes,
Jojo ManaliparampilPrincipal, K.E. Carmel School
