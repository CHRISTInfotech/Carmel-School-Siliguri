An Inspiring Education.
A Community Where You Belong.

K.E. Carmel School, Siliguri is a co-educational English-medium institution committed to academic excellence, character formation and holistic development in a disciplined, caring environment.

The school follows the ICSE curriculum and encourages every learner to grow through academics, creativity, sports, leadership and service.
