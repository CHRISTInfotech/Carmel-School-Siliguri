School Fees Details

  1. The school fees & bus fees cover twelve calendar months and must be paid in advance at the Bank, informed from the school office. One time payment will offer certain rebate.
  2. The fees are to be paid before 10th of each month or the specified date in school calendar failing of which, a late fee of Rs. 50/- will be levied per month. prolonged fee dues beyond one month will attract additional fine.
  3. Pupils have to pay fees until they are withdrawn with a request for Transfer Certificate. Fees once paid are not refundable.
  4. All dues must be cleared before each terminal examination. Those who do not clear the dues will not be permitted to appear for any of the examinations.
  5. Since the final examinations start in the last week of February, students are supposed to clear the February & March fees in February itself, otherwise fine will be applicable.
  6. The management reserves the right to increase the fees at any time of the year if an increment is deemed necessary.
Need assistance? For admission-related queries, parents can visit the school campus or contact the school office during working hours. Our staff will guide you through the process.
